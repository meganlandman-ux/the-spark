No one sparks change alone.

I don't believe meaningful change in education comes from one person or one good idea. It grows from the way students, colleagues and institutions affect one another.

This portfolio is my attempt to make sense of that: where my thinking comes from, how I'm putting it into practice, and where I'm still learning.

I've spent many years working in education, management, online learning and quality assurance. Learning design is the area I'm now developing most deliberately, and this portfolio is honest about that.

“What does this actually mean for the student?”

The question I return to

Whether I'm designing learning, weighing an educational decision or evaluating an institutional process, this is where I come back to. A policy, a platform or a module is only as good as what it changes for the student on the other side of it.
