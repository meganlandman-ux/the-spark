Looking forward

The professional I am becoming.

Still on the way.

Professional development is an ongoing process.

My Personal and Professional Development Plan (PPDP) has helped me reflect on where I am, recognise the areas where I want to grow, and identify practical steps to move forward.

I think of it as a compass for my professional journey. It helps me take ownership of my development, rather than simply allowing my career to unfold around me.

My direction.

I undertook the learning design course to strengthen my knowledge and skills in learning design, so that I could become a more effective leader, contribute meaningfully to my institution, and support the professional development of my colleagues.

I want to keep developing in two connected areas.

Learning design

Strengthening my theoretical knowledge, and applying it more confidently and critically in practice.

Educational leadership

Developing a broader understanding of institutional management, strengthening my strategic leadership, and increasing my ability to influence change.

These aren't separate career paths. They're connected parts of the professional I want to become.

The areas I want to develop.

Five goals from my PPDP, each with a timeframe and a commitment I can measure.

  1. 01

    Academic knowledge and credibility

    Over the next 12 months, I will dedicate at least 30 minutes each week to reading academic research, articles and policy documents on higher education, teaching and learning, and academic leadership. I will identify and document at least six ways this knowledge can inform or improve my professional practice.

    • 12 months
    • 30+ min reading each week
    • At least 6 connections to practice
  2. 02

    Mentorship and institutional leadership

    Within the next three months, I will identify and approach at least one experienced higher education leader who could mentor me. Over the following nine months, I will aim to have at least four mentoring conversations about academic leadership, institutional management and my longer-term career goals.

    • 3 months to approach a mentor
    • 4+ conversations over the following 9 months
  3. 03

    Institutional, financial and operational management

    Over the next 12 months, I will actively seek practical exposure to institutional financial and operational management, and aim to participate in or observe at least three budgeting, financial planning or operational decision-making processes.

    • 12 months
    • 3+ processes joined or observed
  4. 04

    Learning design knowledge and practice

    Over the next 12 months, I will apply at least three learning design principles or theories to my current work. I will evaluate their impact on the learning experience using student feedback, reflection or other available evidence, and document what I have learned.

    • 12 months
    • 3+ principles or theories applied and evaluated
  5. 05

    Strategic leadership and institutional influence

    Within the next three months, I will talk to my manager or relevant institutional leadership about my longer-term professional development goals, and explore what opportunities, support and responsibilities may be available to help me develop as a broader higher education leader. I will review my progress and the opportunities available after six and twelve months.

    • 3 months to start the conversation
    • 6 and 12 months progress reviews

Being realistic.

This development will need to happen alongside my existing responsibilities. My PPDP identifies the barriers I expect: competing workload demands, day-to-day operational responsibilities, limited access to mentors, and limited opportunities to gain experience in other areas.

I've named them because I want to be realistic about what it will take to make progress, rather than simply listing aspirations.
